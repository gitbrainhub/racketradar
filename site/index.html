    What do 4U, G5 and head heavy mean?
    4U
    A lighter racket, about 80 to 84 grams.
    G5
    A smaller grip size. Good for players with smaller hands or who like wrist control.
    Head heavy
    More weight toward the top. Hits harder, a bit slower to swing.
    Head light
    More weight toward the handle. Quicker to swing.
